★ How It Works

Three Simple Steps

From registration to writing your first line of code.

1

Register

Parents sign up their child for free and pick a learning track. No prior experience or equipment required.

2

Join a Group

Kids are placed in small groups by age band and matched with a volunteer instructor near them.

3

Build & Create

Weekly hands-on sessions where every child builds real projects — and becomes a creator of technology.

★ Age Groups

Adapted to Every Age

The same curiosity, two different journeys.

Ages 6 – 9
Junior Coders ⭐

Visual, playful learning with block-based coding, games and creative projects. Short sessions, lots of building, zero pressure.

Ages 10 – 14
Young Builders 🚀

Real text-based programming in Python and JavaScript, electronics kits and first AI experiments — a genuine head start in tech.
