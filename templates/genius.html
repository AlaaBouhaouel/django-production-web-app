About the Competition

National Genius Olympiad

A pinnacle of creativity and brilliance — inviting participants to demonstrate their innovative solutions across a spectrum of disciplines.

This esteemed competition highlights individual ingenuity and fosters collaboration and cross-cultural understanding. Winners earn the opportunity to represent Tunisia on the global stage at the International Genius Olympiad, showcasing their projects to an international audience.

GENIUS Olympiad is an international high school project competition focused on environmental issues, founded by Terra Science and Education and hosted by the Rochester Institute of Technology. Projects span five general disciplines with an environmental focus.
