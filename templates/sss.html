Who We Are

About ATAST

ATAST

Founded in 2007 and officially announced in the JORT in 2012, ATAST has grown into a national force for science, technology, and youth innovation in Tunisia.

Through flagship initiatives like B-Tech, B-Tech Junior, STC, and I²-FEST, ATAST keeps building bridges between education, creativity, and real-world impact.

Student Section

The ATAST Student Section brings together 24 university clubs across different disciplines, creating a nationwide network of ambitious student innovators.

It drives student-focused camps, academies, competitions, and leadership experiences designed to grow technical skills, entrepreneurial spirit, and collaboration.

What WE Do

3 exceptional days filled with innovation, learning, competition, and unforgettable experiences — for 300+ students from 24 ATAST clubs across Tunisia.

Inspiring Conferences

Expert speakers share insights on AI, sustainability, transportation, and cutting-edge technologies.

Interactive Workshops

Hands-on sessions covering practical skills in tech, design thinking, leadership, and entrepreneurship.

B-Tech Competition

The flagship competition where 24 ATAST clubs compete to showcase innovative tech projects aligned with a specific theme.

B-Tech Junior

For school club participants aged 12–18. Same concept as B-Tech, tailored to younger innovators.

Hackathon Challenge

An intense hackathon pushing teams to build creative solutions to real-world problems in limited time.

Student Talent Show

The first edition — a celebration of Tunisian creativity, passion and diversity of talent. Music, dance, theater, spoken word and more.

Round Table Discussions

Engage in deep discussions with experts on the future of technology and its societal impact.

Networking Opportunities

Connect with 300+ students, professionals, and leaders from diverse fields across Tunisia.

Gala Night

An unforgettable evening celebration with awards, entertainment, and lasting memories.

SS Election

The Student Section election — shaping the future leadership of ATAST's student community.

Trophies & Prizes

Valuable prizes, trophies, and the ATAST Labo prize for the B-Tech winner to compete worldwide.
