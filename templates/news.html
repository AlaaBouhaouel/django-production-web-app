Tunisia Qualifies for the International Olympiad on Artificial Intelligence 2026

May 2026  ·  Ksar Hellal, Tunisia

For the first time in its history, ATAST has secured a national qualifier berth at the International Olympiad on Artificial Intelligence (IOAI 2026). Three students from the club network will represent Tunisia on the world stage, competing against teams from over 60 countries.

The qualifying process spanned six months of intensive preparation sessions covering machine learning fundamentals, neural network design, and competitive problem-solving. Coaches credit the club's X-Mind advanced training program as the backbone of the team's readiness.

ATAST Delegation Travels to Portugal for Robotics Championship

March 2024

A six-member team from the Ksar Hellal and Sousse clubs competed at the International Robotics Open in Lisbon, Portugal — the club's first European competition. They placed 4th in the autonomous navigation category.

The trip doubled as a cultural exchange; the delegation met with Portuguese STEM educators and brought back new workshop frameworks that are now being piloted locally.
