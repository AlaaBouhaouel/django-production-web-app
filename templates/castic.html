About the Competition

One of the World's Largest Youth Science Competitions

CASTIC — the China Adolescents Science and Technology Innovation Contest — is organised by the China Association for Science and Technology and the Ministry of Education of China.

With roots dating back to 1982, CASTIC has grown into a massive multi-tiered competition in which more than 10 million students participate through local and provincial rounds each year, with approximately 500 of the strongest projects advancing to the national finals.

Top performers at CASTIC earn direct pathways to prestigious international competitions including the Regeneron ISEF, the EU Contest for Young Scientists, and Broadcom Masters International — making CASTIC one of the most powerful launchpads in global youth science.

ATAST's Role

Tunisia's Pathway to CASTIC

ATAST coordinates Tunisia's participation in CASTIC — identifying, preparing, and officially representing Tunisian student projects in the international tracks of the competition.

Through ATAST's national selection process, the most innovative Tunisian student projects are identified and prepared for submission to CASTIC's international category. ATAST provides mentorship, presentation coaching, and logistical support so that Tunisian teams arrive ready to compete at the highest level.

For Tunisian students, CASTIC is also a direct gateway: outstanding performance can earn qualification for the Regeneron ISEF and the EU Contest for Young Scientists — two of the most prestigious international science competitions in the world.

Project Categories

What Can You Submit?

CASTIC welcomes projects across the full spectrum of science, technology, engineering, and innovation — with a strong emphasis on practical problem-solving and original research.

Natural Sciences

Biology, chemistry, physics, earth science, and environmental research projects based on original experimentation.

Engineering & Technology

Innovative devices, systems, or processes that solve real-world technical problems through creative engineering.

Science & Technology Innovation

Projects that combine scientific knowledge with technological application to deliver a novel, impactful innovation.

Mathematics & Computer Science

Mathematical modelling, algorithms, data science, AI, and software solutions to scientific or social challenges.

Biology & Environmental Science

Research on living systems, ecology, sustainability, and environmental protection with a practical conservation angle.

Creative Invention

Original inventions and practical devices — judged on novelty, feasibility, and the significance of the problem addressed.

How It Works

Eligibility & Requirements

Who Can Apply?

CASTIC is open to high school and pre-university students. Through ATAST, Tunisian students can participate in the international tracks of the competition.

  • Pre-university / high school student
  • Original, independently conducted research or innovation project
  • Project must be in one of the accepted scientific or technological categories
  • Must go through ATAST's national selection process
  • Individual and team projects accepted
  • Strong documentation and presentation are essential

CASTIC — Your Gateway to the World

Competing at CASTIC as a Tunisian student is rare and prestigious. Top CASTIC projects receive direct invitations to Regeneron ISEF (the world's largest pre-college science fair), the EU Contest for Young Scientists in Europe, and Broadcom Masters International — giving CASTIC participants an unparalleled set of doors to open through a single strong project.
