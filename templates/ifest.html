About I-Fest²

I-FEST² is a 9-day festival organized by ATAST — the Tunisian Association for the Future of Science and Technology. 🌍 It is open to all students between the ages of 14 and 24, bringing together young innovators from around the world.

The program is designed to be as unique, educational, and fun as possible — featuring competitions, workshops, and hands-on activities. 🔬⚙️ Participants also enjoy guided trips and excursions to discover the rich Tunisian culture. 🏛️
