How It Works

ISEF finalists are not selected â€” they win their way in. The path from idea to the international stage demands genuine scientific rigour.

01

Original Research

Develop an independent research project in one of 22 categories. Projects must involve no more than 12 months of continuous research conducted within 18 months before the competition.

02

Win the ATAST Qualifier

Submit your project to ATAST's national science fair. Only those who place at the top of the national competition earn the right to represent Tunisia at ISEF.

03

Official Affiliation

ATAST, as a Society for Science affiliated organisation, formally submits Tunisia's selected projects and registers the finalists for the international competition.

04

Finalists travel to the ISEF host city â€” past venues include Los Angeles, Atlanta, and Phoenix. The 2026 event was held at the Phoenix Convention Center.

05

Multi-Round Judging

Nearly 1,000 volunteer PhD-level judges evaluate projects across multiple rounds. Each project is reviewed at least four times by specialists in the relevant field.

06

Awards Ceremony
