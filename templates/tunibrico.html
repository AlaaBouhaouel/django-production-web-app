About TuniBrico

An immersive event designed to ignite curiosity and exploration in STEM among students of all ages.

TuniBrico is a dynamic gathering that equips participants with essential skills in robotics, coding, and scientific exploration. It encourages collaborative learning and the exchange of innovative ideas, fostering a passion for STEM disciplines.

Students engage in hands-on activities that cultivate problem-solving abilities and ignite curiosity about the possibilities within science and technology.
