About Us

Join the Community

ATAST is a Tunisian association dedicated to science and technology, inspiring young minds and nurturing the next generation of STEM leaders.

  • Comprehensive programs in robotics, coding, and emerging technologies to equip students with future-ready skills.
  • Experienced mentors who develop analytical thinking and problem-solving abilities.
  • Hands-on support to turn creative ideas into tangible results.

Join us where the future of science and technology begins.
