FAQ

Before your team deploys

How do we register for ORO competitions?

Use the Register Now button on top. You can also enter through each category page and submit team details from there.

Do teams need prior competition experience?

No. ORO has divisions for different levels. Beginners can start in Poussins or Juniors and grow from season to season.

Can one school register multiple teams?

Yes. Many schools register multiple teams as long as each team follows its category rules and challenge constraints.
